# E2E: share links & permissions

1. Editor TopBar -> click "Share" to open the Share dialog
2. Toggle "Allow full-resolution download", optionally pick expiry, click "Create & copy link"
   ASSERT: new share link appears under "Active share links" and URL copies to clipboard
3. Open /s/:token in a logged-out context (no cookie)
   ASSERT: image renders cleanly with non-destructive adjustments & "Download" button + "Made with Editorium"
4. Click "Open Editorium" link on SharePage -> navigates cleanly to / and bootstraps guest session
5. Back in Share dialog (owner): click "Revoke" on the active link
6. Reload /s/:token
   ASSERT: shows "This link has expired or was revoked."
